## Important SEO limitation

No website can be guaranteed to appear first in Google from on-page optimization alone. Rankings depend on the query, location, competition, content quality, crawl/indexation, site performance, internal/external links, reputation and other signals. The site should be connected to Google Search Console and monitored after deployment.

## Image strategy

Industry pages now use relevant real-world photography rather than generic 3D illustrations. Image `alt` text is descriptive and tied to the page subject. Keep real project screenshots/case-study imagery where it provides stronger proof than stock photography.

## Post-deployment checklist

1. Verify every canonical URL in Search Console.
2. Submit `/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect the home page and every commercial service URL for indexing.
4. Add/verify the Google Business Profile for the real business location and keep NAP details consistent.
5. Publish genuinely useful case studies and industry guides with original evidence.
6. Earn relevant, legitimate links and mentions rather than buying bulk backlinks.
7. Measure impressions, clicks and queries in Search Console and expand pages based on real query data.
